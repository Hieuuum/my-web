Starting a new research project is one of the most disorienting feelings. There is too much to read, too many directions to explore, and no clear signal on what actually matters.

Here is the process that has worked for me.

## Start with one paper

Pick the most cited or most recent paper in the area you care about. Read it slowly. Do not skip the related work section — it is essentially a curated reading list written by experts.

## Build a minimal experiment

Before reading everything, get something running. A toy experiment, a baseline reproduction, anything that gives you a feedback loop. Theory without practice drifts.

## Keep a research log

Write down what you tried, what broke, and what surprised you. Future you will not remember why you made that decision, and your advisor will ask.

## Expect confusion

Confusion is not a sign you are doing it wrong. It is the default state of research. The goal is to be confused about increasingly specific things.
